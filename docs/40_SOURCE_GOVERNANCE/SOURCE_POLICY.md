# Source Policy

**Classification:** AUTHORITATIVE

Marifetname is the primary authority. Selected Ibn Sina material is supplementary only
where the primary is incomplete or unclear. Other classical/Turkish historical sources
require explicit scope, edition, rights, subject, safety, and publication approval.

Lexicons support OCR normalization, historical term matching, lookup, and query expansion;
they are not answer-generation authorities. Academic works support context, bibliography,
and discovery; they are not primary historical sources. Historical astrology is
admin-only/restricted by default and cannot support deterministic personality, fate,
compatibility, health, child, or third-party conclusions.

Modern frameworks such as MBTI, Jung archetypes, Enneagram, Silva, modern personality
tests, Western astrology, and clinical interpretations are excluded as independent
frameworks. A theme present in an approved historical source must remain contextualized
and cited.

Every source requires exact-edition provenance, immutable fingerprint, legal scope,
page/OCR confidence, independent human decisions, approved chunks, and publication
manifest. Unknown means unknown; availability, age, filename, or agent inference is not
evidence. No self-learning or direct internet-to-production path exists.
