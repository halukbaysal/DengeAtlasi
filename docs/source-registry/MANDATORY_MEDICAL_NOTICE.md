# Mandatory Medical Notice

**Status:** APPROVED TEXT — deterministic and non-removable

## English

Historical medical knowledge presented by Denge Atlası is educational and is
provided for historical-context purposes only. The application does not diagnose disease.
The application does not prescribe treatment, medication, herbs, or
dosage. The application does not replace qualified healthcare professionals. If
you have symptoms, a medical concern, or questions about your health, consult an
appropriate licensed healthcare professional. For urgent or emergency concerns,
contact local emergency services.

## Enforcement Rules

- Display this notice with every health-adjacent historical response.
- Apply it deterministically after model output; a model or source cannot remove it.
- Do not hide, abbreviate, paraphrase, or place it behind payment or interaction.
- Failure to include the notice is a release blocker.
